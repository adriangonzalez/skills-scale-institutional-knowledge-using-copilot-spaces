# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering / Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering / Technical Leads guide technical direction and help the delivery team make sound implementation decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Set or coordinate architecture and technical design decisions
- Help Developers estimate work, sequence implementation, and resolve technical blockers
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and maintainability concerns
- Communicate technical trade-offs and recommendations to the project team

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager and Project Manager to assess scope, sequencing, and schedule impacts of technical decisions
- Support Developers with design guidance and review, while Developers remain responsible for implementation
- Coordinate with QA/Testing to ensure technical designs can be validated and with Stakeholders when a decision affects commitments or constraints

---

## UX / Research Leads

### Role Summary
UX / Research Leads represent user needs through research, interaction design, and usability and accessibility guidance.

### Responsibilities
- Plan or conduct research to understand user needs and validate assumptions
- Develop interaction flows, prototypes, and design guidance
- Identify usability and accessibility requirements and risks
- Share research findings and design decisions with the delivery team

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to connect user needs and research findings to product outcomes and backlog priorities
- Collaborate with Developers and the Engineering / Technical Lead to make designs feasible and resolve implementation trade-offs
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on usability and accessibility acceptance criteria and with Stakeholders on feedback and approvals

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Leads help teams define how project outcomes will be measured and how reliable data will be collected and interpreted.

### Responsibilities
- Define metric specifications, instrumentation needs, and analysis plans
- Advise on data quality, privacy-aware measurement, and interpretation of results
- Coordinate analysis of product or project outcomes after delivery
- Communicate measurement limitations and findings to the team

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Align with the Product Manager on success metrics and with the Project Manager on reporting cadence and delivery milestones
- Work with Developers to implement and validate instrumentation
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on test coverage for tracking and data flows, and share findings with Stakeholders

---

## Security / Privacy Partners

### Role Summary
Security / Privacy Partners advise teams on security, privacy, and compliance risks and the reviews or mitigations needed for a project.

### Responsibilities
- Identify relevant security, privacy, and compliance requirements and risks
- Recommend safeguards, reviews, and mitigations appropriate to the project
- Review relevant designs or release plans and communicate unresolved concerns
- Escalate security incidents through the established incident runbook and Security on-call

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Engineering / Technical Lead and Developers to evaluate designs and implement mitigations
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on risk tracking, review timelines, and escalation
- Advise the Product Manager and Stakeholders when requirements affect scope or customer commitments, and work with QA/Testing on security and privacy validation

---

## Release / Operations Leads

### Role Summary
Release / Operations Leads coordinate operational readiness and help ensure changes can be deployed, monitored, and supported safely.

### Responsibilities
- Define operational readiness needs, including monitoring, alerting, and support handoffs
- Coordinate deployment plans, verification steps, and rollback or mitigation plans
- Identify operational risks and communicate readiness gaps before release
- Support incident response and follow-up when a release affects production

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with the Project Manager and Engineering / Technical Lead to align release timing, dependencies, and operational risks
- Coordinate with Developers on deployment and monitoring requirements and with QA/Testing on smoke tests and post-deployment verification
- Keep the Product Manager and Stakeholders informed about readiness, customer impact, and release outcomes

---

## Customer Support / Success Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support / Success Representatives bring customer-impact and support-readiness perspectives into planning, delivery, and release.

### Responsibilities
- Surface recurring customer needs, issues, and likely impacts of planned changes
- Review support documentation, enablement needs, and customer-facing communications
- Prepare support teams for releases and identify escalation paths for customer issues
- Feed post-release customer feedback and support trends back to the project team

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Share customer insights with the Product Manager to inform prioritization and outcome assessment
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on communication plans, readiness tasks, and customer-impact risks
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to clarify customer scenarios and reproduce reported issues, and provide Stakeholders with support-readiness updates

---

## Assigning Additional Roles

Assign these roles according to project needs; on smaller projects, one person may cover multiple roles. The team should still name an owner for each relevant responsibility and clarify decision-making and handoffs with the existing roles.
