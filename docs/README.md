## OctoAcme project management processes

OctoAcme uses an iterative, customer-focused lifecycle. Projects begin by validating the business need, identifying stakeholders, defining success measures, and deciding whether to proceed. Planning turns approved initiatives into prioritized, estimable work with acceptance criteria, milestones, a Definition of Done, and documented risks and dependencies. During execution, teams coordinate through standups, delivery syncs, project boards, small reviewed pull requests, and automated and manual quality checks; blockers are escalated through defined levels. Risks and stakeholder communications are reviewed regularly. Releases follow pre-release checks, deployment and verification steps, and rollback or incident guidance. Teams close the loop through retrospectives, tracking a small set of improvement actions. Project Managers coordinate delivery, Product Managers own outcomes and prioritization, and developers, QA, and stakeholders contribute to implementation and validation.
